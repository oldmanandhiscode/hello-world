# Personal Trainer Platform Blueprint

## Personal Introduction & Purpose
This platform is a personal accountability and transformation tool designed to document, visualise, and optimise a return to peak fitness. It focuses on clear metric tracking, powerful visualisation, consistency reinforcement, and adaptability to evolving goals. The same foundation is intended to scale into an online personal training business that delivers a premium, branded experience for 20–40+ clients with strong accountability and coaching efficiency.

## Vision
A lightweight but full coaching platform for onboarding, accountability, progress tracking, and delivery of personalised programming and education. It starts as a personal tool, then scales easily for multiple clients, prioritising data, visuals, and behavioural change over feature bloat.

## Strategic Rationale
- Current platforms emphasise workouts; few excel at visualisation and accountability.
- A proprietary system differentiates coaching and elevates perceived value.
- Modular components keep development manageable and scalable.
- The platform acts as the trainer’s operating system, integrating education, tracking, and client management without unnecessary complexity.
- Starting with the trainer as the first client ensures features solve real needs.

## Core Principles
- **Modular**: Components can evolve independently.
- **Lightweight**: Only essential functions are included.
- **Trainer-Centric**: Efficiently supports 20–40+ clients.
- **Client-Friendly**: Simple, clear, and motivating UI.
- **Scalable**: Data model and flows support growth from 1 to many clients.
- **Evidence-Driven**: Focus on metrics and behaviours that drive results.

## MVP Scope (Version 1)
The MVP must be usable immediately, simple to maintain solo, robust enough for 5–10 clients, and expandable without major rework. It prioritises progress tracking, onboarding, and accountability with lightweight training and nutrition components.

### 1) Onboarding Module (Coach + Client)
Capture goals, motivations, starting metrics/photos, training history, lifestyle, preferences, and constraints, ending with a structured “Client Profile & Starting Point” summary.

### 2) Progress & Metrics Module (Customisable)
Configurable measurement profile per client with core metrics (weight, waist, chest, hips, optional bodyfat) and optional metrics (neck, shoulders, limbs, HR, BP, steps, sleep, stress, energy, menstrual phase, etc.). Includes logging, editing/back-dating, quick log, graphs (line, 7-day moving average, trend line, deltas), and dashboard KPIs with autogenerated insights.

### 3) Progress Photos & Visual Change Module
Supports multiple photo categories with enable/disable per client, guided uploads, galleries, filters, lightbox, “start vs now” shortcuts, before/after slider, crossfade, optional morph effect, and future AI-assisted alignment/normalisation.

### 4) Training Module (Lite)
Defines training blocks and assigned workouts with exercises, sets/reps/tempo/rest, cues, substitutions, and linked demos. Client view emphasises phase guidance over detailed logging.

### 5) Nutrition Module (Lite)
Sets calorie/macro targets (phase-aware), example days, snack ideas, shopping lists, and simple compliance logging (on-target/over/under or manual macros).

### 6) Weekly Check-In Module
Structured client form (metrics, photos, sleep/stress/energy, compliance, wins, challenges, coach questions) and coach view with trend snapshot, auto insight summary, and space for feedback/next-week plan.

### 7) Coach Dashboard
Client list with goals, status, latest updates, filters, quick actions (macros, training block switch, nudges, notes), and drill-down to full profiles. Flags overdue check-ins or missing data.

### 8) Content Library
Educational materials across training, nutrition, mindset, lifestyle, and onboarding guidance, supporting text, PDFs, videos, and checklists.

## Product Roadmap
- **Phase 0 — Foundation**: Define metrics, photo categories, onboarding questions, and data structures; decide measurement defaults.
- **Phase 1 — MVP Build**: Onboarding flow, metrics logging + basic graphs (weight/waist), photo upload + gallery, before/after slider, basic coach dashboard.
- **Phase 2 — Accountability Engine**: Weekly check-ins, compliance tracking, automated trend insights, alerts, expanded graphs/KPIs.
- **Phase 3 — Coaching Tools & Content**: Training block library, nutrition targets/examples, content library, client notes/tags.
- **Phase 4 — Multi-Client Optimisation**: Better dashboard filters/search, semi-automated weekly summaries, onboarding templates.
- **Phase 5 — Experience Polishing**: Mobile UX/UI refinements, improved photo tools, client QoL features.
- **Phase 6 — Optional Business Expansion**: Multi-coach support, white-labelling/licensing, advanced morphing and AI alignment.

## High-Level Data Model (Conceptual)
- **Entities**: Coach, Client, Measurement Entry, Photo, Training Block, Nutrition Target, Check-In, Content Item, Note.
- **Relationships**: Coach has many clients; client has many measurement entries, photos, and check-ins; clients are assigned training blocks and nutrition targets over time.

## Success Criteria
- Improves personal adherence and clarity.
- Reduces coaching admin and highlights who needs attention.
- Motivates clients and supports 20–40 active clients comfortably.
- Forms a strong backbone for a scalable, high-quality online coaching business.
